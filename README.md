# JCs_molbio

Journal club slide decks for molecular biology, built with [Quarto](https://quarto.org) (`revealjs`).

## RNA, protein and cell state — What does a correlation measure?

A partial review of RNA–protein correlation, measurement accuracy and same-cell proteomics, centered on:

> Thielert et al., *In the same cell, the proteome defines cellular state and the transcriptome marks transitions*, bioRxiv v1 (2026), doi:10.64898/2026.08.25.746940

and historical evidence from Gygi et al. (1999), Elowitz et al. (2002), Raj et al. (2006), Taniguchi et al. (2010), Schwanhäusser et al. (2011), Li, Bickel & Biggin (2014), and Csárdi et al. (2015).

- Source: `index.qmd`
- Rendered slides: `index.html` (self-contained, open directly in a browser)
- Figures: `images/` (cropped panels from the original papers, used for journal-club discussion with attribution)

### Build

```bash
quarto render index.qmd
```
